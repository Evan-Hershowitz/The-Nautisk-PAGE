Tidepool — A Nautisk Sub-brand

The news,
made for you.

Tidepool explains the stuff that actually matters — money, science, college life, and the world — for high school and college readers who want real information without the complexity wall.

What Tidepool covers

The main Nautisk covers Finance, Law, Healthcare, Politics, and Tech for a general adult audience. Tidepool covers four different lenses — Money, Science, College, and World — and writes for a reader who is smart and curious but doesn't have years of background in these topics yet.

Conversational, not dumbed-down

We assume you're intelligent. We don't assume you're an expert. There's a difference, and Tidepool is built around it.

600–900 words

Long enough to actually explain something. Short enough that you'll read to the end. No padding, no filler.

Four categories

Money, Science, College, and World — one fresh article per category per week, on topics you'd actually want to read about.

Real sourcing

Every Tidepool article is based on real reporting — studies, data, and sources you can check. Accessible writing doesn't mean unchecked writing.
